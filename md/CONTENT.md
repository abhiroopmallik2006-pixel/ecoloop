Copy below is proposed, not a claim of an operating service. [PRD](PRD.md) defines the actual scope; [SEO](SEO.md) limits public claims.

**Hero:** “Predict surplus. Put resources to better use.”

**How it works:** “Measure what is happening. Forecast what may happen next. Review a recommended action. Record the result.”

**Three layers:**

- “A modular hub: connect the sensors and resource systems your site needs.”
- “A shared workspace: review forecasts, resource passports, handoffs, and rewards.”
- “A growing network: a planned way to connect suitable surplus with nearby demand.”

## Claims and labels

Use “measured,” “estimated,” “predicted,” “verified by [authorized role],” and “simulated” precisely. “Verified” refers to specified evidence/review, not universal regulatory approval. Use “water reused” for recorded reuse; use “estimated freshwater displacement” only when supported by a method. “Solar generated” does not imply “grid avoided.” Do not call all compost output avoided landfill mass.

Do not publish unverified percentages, customers, partner logos, testimonials, launch dates, prices, emissions savings, India-first claims, or claims that plants improve clinical health/air quality. Figures in demo fixtures must be labeled illustrative. GreenPoints have no cash value in P0. No statement of WCAG/legal compliance until reviewed and supported.

## Assets and language

Prefer an original simple loop mark, stream icons, and an accessible static resource-flow illustration. Photography requires license/provenance and consent where identifiable people appear; avoid presenting stock photos as actual installations. Decorative images have empty alt text; meaningful diagrams need text equivalents. Keep source/license records for all assets.

Write plain English with Indian operational context; expand RWA on first use. Store messages as localization keys, separate units/numbers from grammar, and use Intl with site timezone. Do not concatenate translated fragments. Avoid emoji as the sole signal and jargon such as digital twin on primary operator controls.

## Content governance

Product owner approves marketing claims; site safety lead approves module instructions; technical owner approves methodology; privacy owner approves notices. Store approved revisions with date and reviewer in repository history. User-entered notes are untrusted text and must never become executable prompts or unsanitized markup.
